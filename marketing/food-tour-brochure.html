Hội An
Food Tour

7 dishes, 3km through the Old Town's back alleys and street stalls — with a local guide who knows every vendor by name.

The Menu
7 Stops, 7 Dishes
01Há Cảo — White Rose Dumplings
Steamed rice dumplings shaped like blooming roses, filled with shrimp and pork, topped with crispy fried shallots.
02Bánh Bèo — Water Fern Cake
Small steamed rice cakes topped with shrimp, pork and crispy noodles, eaten with a tiny bamboo blade.
03Bánh Bột Lọc
Chewy, translucent tapioca dumplings filled with shrimp and pork belly, sometimes wrapped in banana leaf.
04Xí Mà Phú — Black Sesame Soup
Warm, glossy black sesame dessert soup with a nutty flavor, sold from traditional street vendors.
05Cao Lầu
Hội An's signature noodle dish — thick rice noodles, pork and crispy croutons, made with water from a single well.
06Bánh Căn
Mini savory rice pancakes grilled in terracotta molds over charcoal, served with fish sauce.
07Tropical Mixed Fruit Smoothie
A cold blended smoothie of fresh local tropical fruit — the sweet finish to the walk.
