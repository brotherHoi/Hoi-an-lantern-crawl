FAQ

Everything you were about to ask

Drinks, meeting point, payment, cancellation, rain, dietary needs — answered in full. Still stuck? Message us on WhatsApp, we reply fast.

Is there a drink limit? What happens if someone gets too drunk?

At each stop covered by the tour, every guest gets 1 drink or 1 beer included. Extra drinks are paid by the guest. If a guest gets too drunk, the guide will help arrange a trusted taxi and brief the driver, so the guest doesn't get overcharged. If a guest blacks out, the tour covers the taxi cost.

Where's the meeting point? Do you offer hotel pickup?

There's a fixed meeting point — you'll find the details on our website and tour listing. We don't currently offer hotel or hostel pickup.

Will I be grouped with strangers? What's the right age for this tour?

Yes, groups are combined with other bookings. There's no age or gender restriction — just be 18 or over and up for a good time.

How far in advance do I need to book? What's the cancellation policy?

Book by 7 PM on the day of the tour. Free 100% cancellation if you cancel before 3 PM on the day of the tour.

How and when do I pay?

Currently cash only, or bank transfer if you have a Vietnamese bank account. Payment is made at the meeting point before the tour starts. Online payment with early-booking discounts is coming soon.

Does the tour run in the rain?

Yes — light rain won't stop the party. If it rains heavily, the itinerary flexes to keep things safe and fun: swapping the electric buggy for taxis if the group is under 7 people, or hopping between bars near the Old Town instead of heading to the beach. In case of storms or flooding, the tour is cancelled for safety.

How much walking is involved? Is it suitable for older guests or high heels?

The tour involves about 1.5km of walking in total. If you're not used to walking regularly, please keep that in mind before joining.

Is the food vegetarian-friendly or safe for allergies?

The main dish is Vietnamese-style BBQ served with peanut sauce. If you're vegetarian or have any food allergies, please let us know in advance so we can arrange a suitable option.

Can I join if I don't drink alcohol?

Absolutely. We always have non-alcoholic drink options available, so you can enjoy Hội An's nightlife and make new friends without drinking.

Does the price include a tip? Is there a discount for larger groups?

The price doesn't include a tip for the guide. We don't currently offer group discounts, as our pricing already reflects our actual costs — this may change in the future.

What happens after the tour ends? Where can I leave feedback?

Your guide stays with the group until the scheduled end time, then may head home or stick around and keep the party going with you. After the tour you'll receive a short feedback survey via WhatsApp or social media. If you enjoyed it, we'd love a review on GetYourGuide, TripAdvisor or Google Maps. If anything wasn't up to standard, message us directly on WhatsApp so we can fix it quickly.
