The Menu

7 dishes on the walk

Há Cảo (White Rose Dumplings) Steamed rice dumplings shaped like blooming roses, filled with shrimp and pork, topped with crispy fried shallots.
Bánh Bèo (Water Fern Cake) Small steamed rice cakes topped with shrimp, pork and crispy noodles, eaten with a tiny bamboo blade.
Bánh Bột Lọc Chewy, translucent tapioca dumplings filled with shrimp and pork belly, sometimes wrapped in banana leaf.
Xí Mà Phú (Black Sesame Soup) Warm, glossy black sesame dessert soup with a nutty flavor, sold from traditional street vendors.
Cao Lầu Hội An's signature dry noodles, made with Ba Lễ well water and Cham Island ash, topped with barbecue pork.
Bánh Căn Mini savory rice pancakes grilled in terracotta molds over charcoal, served with fish sauce.
Tropical Mixed Fruit Smoothie A cold blended smoothie of fresh local tropical fruit, the sweet finish to the walk.
